- Eradicated all neon cyber-glows, dark fluorescent buttons, and generic robotic chatbots.
- Standardized on the authoritative **Oxford Academic Navy** (`#0A2540`), **Scholarly Pure Paper White** (`#FFFFFF`), and **Warm Ivory** (`#FAFAF8`).
- Physical 3D tactile button depth across all core operational workflows:

  - `"টিউশন ফি রিলিজ করুন"` (Guardian Trust Escrow)
  - `"উত্তর নিশ্চিত করুন"` & `"পরীক্ষা সমাপ্ত করুন"` (Digital Exam Hall)

  - `"প্রতিকারমূলক পাঠ শুরু করো"` (Curriculum Knowledge Graph)
  - `"লোকাল কুইজ শুরু করুন"` & `"ক্লাউডে সিঙ্ক করুন"` (Offline Classroom Mesh)

### II. Sovereign Student Privacy & Non-Punitive Proctoring
- **Zero Facial Video Surveillance**: Strictly prohibits webcam streaming, pupil tracking, or biometric recording.
- **Client Heuristic Telemetry**: Monitors non-punitive event heuristics (`visibilitychange`, `blur` duration, audio peaks).
- **Cryptographic SHA-256 Seal**: Mints an immutable, salted hash verification receipt upon exam completion.

### III. Inclusive Offline-First Infrastructure
- **Offline P2P Classroom Mesh**: Enables rural classrooms with up to 60 students to participate in local quizzes over Wi-Fi / Bluetooth using a Lamport-clock CRDT mutation ledger.
- **Rural LoRaWAN Emergency Bridge**: Solar-powered packet micro-hubs ensure continuity during regional flood disruptions or power outages.

### IV. NCTB Board Archives & Mathematical Accuracy
- Authentic past board papers (2015-2025) for Dhaka, Chittagong, Rajshahi, Comilla, Sylhet, Barisal, Jessore, Dinajpur, and Mymensingh boards.
- 100% KaTeX LaTeX equation rendering ensuring sub-pixel mathematical precision.

### V. Collaborative Peer-Review & Democratic Governance
- Dual-examiner blind consensus editorial workflow preventing unilateral AI or human errors from entering live question banks.
- Emergency quarantine switch allowing immediate isolation of compromised questions with audit trails.
